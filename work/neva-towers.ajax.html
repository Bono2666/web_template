Neva Towers

Visit Website

The Neva Towers are futuristic high-rise buildings designed by Ant Yapı, an international development and construction company. Ant Yapı approached us to create two websites: a high-profile promotional site and an internal site for the sales office to help customers select apartments.

Implemented in a functional and minimalist style, Neva Towers website helped affirm the project's leading position in the market. A special website we created for the sales office provides a unique experience that allows the sales representative to select and present the most appropriate offer for the customer.

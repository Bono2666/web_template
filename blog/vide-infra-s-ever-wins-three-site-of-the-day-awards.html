Vide Infra’s ’Ever’ Wins Three ’Site of the Day’ Awards

View Case

August 2023

Real estate, Awards

2 min reading

Our work for "Ever" has achieved a remarkable feat by receiving not one, not two, but three major awards in the web design and development industry. The site has been awarded ‘Website of the Day' by CSSDA, 'Site of the Day' by Awwwards, and also earned the coveted 'Site of the Day' accolade from The FWA. This triple crown achievement solidifies "Ever’s" standing as an absolute champion in the realm of web design and development — a testament to our team's dedication and expertise.

The CSS Design Awards (CSSDA) is an international web design and development award platform that celebrates incredible feats achieved in web design and development. Receiving recognition from CSSDA speaks volumes about a project's creativity, innovation, and usability. An award from this institution is an acknowledgement that a design has truly pushed boundaries and set itself apart in a saturated digital realm.

Awwwards has established its niche as a platform that recognizes the talent and effort of the best web designers, developers, and agencies in the world. An Awwwards recognition is an acknowledgement from the global design community that a design not only meets but goes beyond the standards of user experience, creativity, and content.

The Favourite Website Awards (FWA) is one of the most long-standing and respected institutions in the field of web design and development, recognizing truly advanced and innovative digital solutions. Typically, the awards from FWA are reserved for projects backed by large budgets from big-name companies, underscoring the exceptional quality and innovative nature of the awarded websites.

What makes ‘Ever’ special

Strategy

The idea was to bring to life the key brand’s idea — the harmony between urban chic and natural serenity that perfectly captures the essence of “Ever’s” identity. Our storytelling approach showcases this convergence and transports users into the heart of “Ever”. We structured the website in a way that offers users an effortless yet captivating exploration of the project. We prioritized clarity to ensure that every piece of content, from vibrant, expansive visuals to smaller, intricate details, was easily accessible and provided an immersive experience on the website.

Design Language

Our approach to design is always anchored in authenticity. With "Ever," we embraced a rich, genuine design palette that mirrors the project's core values. The result? A digital space that feels both premium and personal.

3D Renders and Video

To further elevate user engagement, we implemented robust 3D visualizations and videos that serve as digital narratives and offer users a tangible sense of the Ever complex's elegance, further deepening their emotional connection.

Technical Finesse

Beneath the surface, "Ever" boasts of state-of-the-art technical implementation. Creative front-end solutions and smooth animations contribute to the user's journey, making every interaction memorable.

One of our strengths at Vide Infra is crafting distinctive websites for the real estate sector. A deep understanding of this market, coupled with our mastery of design, empowers us to create projects that are both rich and unique. "Ever" is emblematic of our capability to strike a harmonious balance — a site that's vibrant and original yet fully aligns with the client's vision. By deeply understanding the brand, we've woven an emotional and captivating narrative that not just tells but shows what makes "Ever" truly special.

But luxury real estate website design is not our only field of expertise. Our team excels at designing top-notch user experiences across multiple sectors, including luxury eСommerce design, B2B e-commerce design, and UX in banking and fintech. The broad experience we have accumulated across these domains has given us a deep understanding of the unique challenges and opportunities they each offer and allows us to craft tailored solutions that resonate with target audiences.

Having "Ever" recognized by CSSDA, Awwwards and FWA simultaneously is no small feat. It is a testament to the passion and expertise of our design team. At Vide Infra, we've always believed in the pursuit of excellence. Our goal isn't just to create designs but to craft experiences that matter, that speak to people, and that stand the test of time.

Every recognition we receive is a milestone in our ongoing journey. It tells us where we stand and where we need to head next. We're thankful for these moments of acknowledgement, not just for the pride they instill but also for the inspiration they provide.

To our team, who persistently pursue perfection; to our clients, who trust our vision; and to the design community that continues to inspire and challenge us: Thank you. With your support, we move forward, eager to explore and deliver creative and original digital design solutions.

Vide Infra nominated for the Agency of the Year 2017 by CSSDA

February 2018

News

1 min reading

CSS Design Awards, one of the few universally recognized web design competitions, has nominated Vide Infra for the Designer Of The Year 2017 annual award. The nomination reflects Vide Infra’s achievements in the contest and places the agency among the top 27 web design companies in the world.

CSS Design Awards is one of four globally respected web design competitions, with AWWWARDS, The FWA, and the Webby Awards being the other three. A judge panel composed of renowned industry experts rates websites that agencies submit from all over the world. Each day, one winner is selected for the Website Of The Day award. Agencies that win the largest number of awards make it to the Designer Of The Year selection. This year, Vide Infra won four awards with House at Khlebny, Transact Pro, Loftec, and our own website.

“We are thrilled to be nominated as it indicates our capacity for continuously producing top-grade work that finds appreciation not only among the clients but also our professional community. It’s our dedication to the highest creative and technical standards that makes it possible for us to compete with the best companies around the globe” — comments Vide Infra’s creative director and CEO Anton Sulsky.

Other than being nominated as Designer Of The Year by CSS Design Awards, AWWWARDS nominated Vide Infra this year for the Annual Awards in the Best Agency category; a remarkable achievement for the independent creative design boutique from Riga, Latvia.

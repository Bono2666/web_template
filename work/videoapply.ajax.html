VideoApply is an innovative digital product developed in-house by Vide Infra. It is a streamlined one-way interview service. VideoApply dramatically simplifies recruitment, especially for start-ups and small businesses. We started this project from scratch, acting as designers, developers, and marketers, all at once. The result is a simple and effective solution that helps entrepreneurs and companies around the globe find their perfect talent.

Video Interview Platform

After employers post a job on VideoApply, they share the link. Candidates can simply follow it to instantly record a video response. The application page is very friendly and intuitive, with vacancy details on the left side and an application form on the right.

We have broken down the candidate registration process into small, easy steps. This ensures a seamless experience, allowing candidates to create accounts quickly.

Recording video responses is a crucial part of the application process, and we have taken great care to create an exceptional interface for it. It has been thoughtfully designed to minimise stress for candidates, allowing them unlimited attempts while ensuring the privacy of all unsuccessful recordings. This empowers candidates to put their best foot forward.

VideoApply is fully functional on mobile devices. It’s extremely convenient for both applicants and employers.

There are some Easter eggs on error pages to cheer up users. Just in case.

While candidate convenience is essential, our clients are employers. We have focused on giving them the best experience and helping them save time and effort in the hiring process.

The dashboard provides essential statistics and offers a comprehensive overview that helps manage a subscription.

The job posting form is designed to be straightforward. It features only the essential fields and includes question templates.

All company vacancies are collected on one page for easy management. They can be saved as drafts, archived, or republished.

Employers can review and analyse candidate responses with a meticulously optimised interface. It resembles familiar video-sharing platforms. For example, it features like and dislike buttons that make sorting easier.

Messages to candidates can be sent directly from the platform. Employers can send templates to several candidates at once or communicate individually.

There are four subscription plans tailored to fit various business needs, including a free one, which allows users to explore VideoApply.

VideoApply allows employers to create recruitment teams and set access levels for every member.

Promo Website

We are very experienced with promo websites, and of course, we did our best to make an outstanding landing for our own project. Captivating design and thoughtful content highlight all the platform's benefits and invite users to explore VideoApply.

Email Marketing

We have also thought through various communication scenarios and created relevant email sequences. We motivate them to use VideoApply, explain how to do it efficiently, and encourage them to subscribe or stay subscribed.

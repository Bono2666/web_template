Conmet

Conmet researches and produces high-tech medical implants. Despite its industry-leading innovations, the company lacked an appropriate online presence, which caused it to blend into the competitive landscape. They approached us to design and develop a corporate website that matches their cutting-edge products.

Conmet required more than just a medical website design; they needed a systemic brand reboot. Our work involved conducting in-depth research, developing a positioning strategy, refreshing the visual identity, crafting content, and creating a comprehensive company website.

Through a market analysis and a series of workshops with the Conmet team, we gained insight into the industry landscape, customer expectations, product differentiation, and company values. This groundwork allowed us to develop an effective corporate website strategy tailored to Conmet’s brand.

We came up with a corporate website design idea that conveys reliability and innovation. The immersive 3D animation on the homepage, which was made with WebGL, is a design element that embodies this concept. The geometric layouts, minimal color palette, and bold accents contribute to the image of a modern, highly professional, pioneering company.

We distilled the technical details of Conmet’s sophisticated products into a clear, benefit-focused presentation. Segmentation was key: we crafted specific content for medical professionals and patients. The website UI/UX design was developed to serve both audiences, making it easy to access relevant information while supporting the emotional tone.

Conmet’s product touches on the sensitive topic of health and physicality. It is important for healthcare website design to convey empathy and concern. Customers may hesitate to entrust their health to a brand with a strict, industrial style. To address this, we use imagery that highlights Conmet’s commitment to improving quality of life, featuring professionals at work and satisfied clients.

As a web design business, we understand the importance of a thorough strategy for corporate website development. Companies focused on providing high-quality products, such as Conmet, often lack the in-house resources to develop extensive marketing materials. Thanks to our expertise, we can deliver a strategic, visually rich, and emotionally resonant company site almost from scratch, and the Conmet corporate website is a perfect example.

    FWA of The Day

This project is our meditation on the future of mobile app UI and UX design in the fintech sector. We challenged ourselves to imagine how AI tools will transform mobile banking, outlining our ideas in a cutting-edge presentation. The website breaks the mould — it’s a fusion of artistic expression and strategic marketing, crafted to both inspire and promote our UX/UI design services.

Built with WebGL, the entire presentation flows like a narrative. One continuous scroll through inspiring visuals immerses users in a seamless, futuristic 3D environment.

Each screen showcases an idea for a banking mobile app UI design, expressed through minimalist, interactive 3D compositions. Users can explore these concepts and view animated mockups of features that have been crafted specifically for the project.

Despite the rich and complex animations, the site runs smoothly across devices. We paid special attention to optimize the mobile version, keeping vivid animations and transitions.

The central CTA leads to our deep-dive article on AI in banking UX design. We didn’t just visualize features — we conceptualized a full set of innovative and practical functions. Since the article's publication, some of these features have become part of everyday life, proving that our instincts as digital product designers were on point.

We are a web and mobile app development company with a passion for creativity. We love bringing bold, artistic ideas to life and we are glad that our enthusiasm is shared across the professional community. The website was honored with awards from CSSDA, Awwwards, and FWA — global benchmarks in digital design excellence.

We Won a Webby — the Oscars of Web Design

April 2026

Awards, Startup

3 min reading

Not all honors are created equal. Professional web design competitions like Awwwards and CSS Design Awards run daily, with dozens of projects competing for the Site of the Day award. The Webby is different. It is an annual, world-class award in which a demanding jury selects winners across a handful of categories from thousands upon thousands of submissions. The companies entering the Webby include some of the biggest names on the planet. Apple, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Netflix, and NASA, among them. The Webby is, without exaggeration, the Oscar of web design. And now it belongs to us.

We won a Webby in the Best Visual Design — Function category for FOLLOW.ART — a digital network designed for curators and artists to manage professional visibility, collaboration, and networking. We helped build the product from the ground up — crafting the brand identity, streamlining the storytelling, developing the product design, UX, and technical execution. 

Our complete immersion in every aspect of the SaaS startup’s design and development enabled us to achieve synergy between communication and product design, with each part contributing to the success of the whole in distinct ways. The result is an original product that is not only intuitive and easy to use but also inspiring, igniting creative drive and imagination in its users — an art community seeking new ways to connect, collaborate, and grow. 

The win in the Best Functional Design category is especially remarkable because it signifies our ability not only to provide innovative communication and creative design but to solve a functional business challenge. Helping our clients win and retain customers in the most effective way — both in brand communication and product design — is the primary goal of our involvement in every project. It’s rewarding to see this recognized.

A website we developed for Madar, the prominent logistics SaaS startup from Saudi Rabia, was another contender for Webby’s Best Use of Animation or Motion Graphics. Madar made it to the final five nominees in the category — the top five websites selected from the hundreds best contenders in the world. 

We submitted two of our works to the Webbys this year. Both became nominees, and one won a Webby. This is huge! It shows that we can consistently deliver top-quality projects in creative and interaction design, innovation, and technical implementation. We’re proud to have delivered not only outstanding solutions for our clients, but also the spotlight of such a major award.

Anton Sulsky CEO, Vide Infra

The Webby places the quality of our work on par with that of the world's largest, most advanced, and innovative companies. It confirms that a small but highly driven and skilled team committed to helping its clients can outperform even the biggest market leaders. Beyond our own achievement, this is also a victory for the FOLLOW.ART team — a powerful validation of their product and a level of visibility, attention, and trust that money can’t buy. It is yet another affirmation that our vision of improving the world through beautiful and efficient design holds true.

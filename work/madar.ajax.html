This elaborate corporate website makes a statement: Madar isn’t just another SaaS startup — it’s a game-changing product redefining the logistics industry. The company helps businesses automate and streamline their truck shipping operations. Based in the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, Madar currently serves the Gulf region and plans to expand internationally. When Madar approached us, the brief was ambitious: develop a corporate website that reflects their pioneering status on a global level with a cutting-edge design.

Madar is a startup with a sophisticated SaaS product and conservative corporate clients. Our priority was to develop a website with a clear, concise message that explains the product and highlights its key advantages.

The core of the corporate website is a state-of-the-art homepage presentation powered by advanced WebGL animation. WebGL enables 3D rendering directly in the browser, allowing for high-end visual effects and dynamic compositions. Developing this interactive 3D homepage experience required extensive effort and close collaboration between our creative and development teams.

The homepage design is visually striking. It consistently communicates the product’s key benefits through energetic and futuristic visuals — engaging clients emotionally while persuading them with clear, structured arguments.

The website’s structure, content, and functionality are rooted in thorough research. Through a series of workshops with the client team and comprehensive market analysis, we developed a website strategy that aligns all elements of the site with business goals, ensuring both creative consistency and effectiveness.

Behind the homepage lies a robust content framework: a detailed product explanation, a high-end company presentation, and case studies. These pages help customers quickly find logistics solutions relevant to their needs while reinforcing Madar’s image as a reliable partner and employer.

A modern, thoughtfully designed user interface supports this content. Inspiring photography, custom video, icons, infographics, and microanimations create a seamless, immersive user experience. The website’s UI/UX quality communicates the promise of Madar’s product quality.

To create this vivid and emotionally resonant website, we significantly extended the brand’s visual system. The original brand materials were limited, so we used the core colors and graphic motifs as a foundation to build out a richer and more expressive visual language.

As a web design agency for startups, we always ensure top-tier implementation quality. The Madar website is optimized for full functionality across mobile and desktop devices. The visuals automatically adapt to device performance, ensuring a consistently smooth experience.

The result is international recognition. The website was awarded Site of the Day by both CSSDA and FWA — two of the world’s leading web design festivals. Most importantly, it now inspires Madar’s clients, partners, and team to work together — the ultimate goal of our collaboration.

AQRM

AQRM is an investment management and advisory firm with a strong focus on emerging markets, FX and precious metals. The company approached us for a custom investor portfolio analytics system development. Together with the AQRM team, we've created a unique product that makes sophisticated portfolio management and analysis easy.

The system allows for consolidating and analysing asset portfolios, forecasting performance, and emulating the addition of various assets. We've created an authentic UX and UI specifically for the product and developed the system's front-end and backend.
